# AI No-Show Rescue Demo - Interactive Demo

Interactive demo: an AI SMS reminder rescues an appointment before it becomes a no-show. The visitor plays a patient of fictional Willow Creek Dental Studio, receives a reminder text, and picks Confirm, Reschedule or Cancel. Every branch ends on a saved or rebooked appointment, with a live seconds timer and a booking CTA.

Scripted simulation: fictional business and patient, fake 555 number, no real messages, calls or bookings.
